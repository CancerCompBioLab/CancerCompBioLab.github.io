# Welcome to the Cancer Computational Biology group site.


The Cancer Computational Biology lab at VHIO leverage epi(genetic) cancer datasets in order to discover the molecular mechanism related with cancer initiation, progression, drug resistance and metastasis in order to improve patient perspectives.

## Targeting the un-targetable
We are interested in the role of chromatin regulatory genes as a synthetic lethality tool to target tumor suppressor or oncogenes using the synthetic lethality paradigm.
## Epigenetic mechanism of drug resistance
We want to investigate what are the epigenetic changes behind a successful first line treatment.  
## Personalized medicine
We aim to develop tools that allow us to identify which patient will get benefit for a specific drug or drug combination.
